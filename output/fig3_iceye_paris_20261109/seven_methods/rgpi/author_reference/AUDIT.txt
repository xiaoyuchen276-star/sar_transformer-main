RGPI author-program comparison

Paper-confirmed geometry: opposite 3x3 block centers at (+/-2,0), (0,+/-2), (+/-2,+/-2); full 7x7 support. Figure 1 is saved.
Paper equation (18) defines input-derived heterogeneity weight and text says weights in [0,1]. The paper does not explicitly specify variance ddof, border padding, or denominator adjustment after omitted zero-containing direction pairs.
The project equation implementation documents ddof=1, negative weights clipped to 0, valid 3-pixel border, and valid direction-pair denominator. These are implementation conventions, not verified internals of the author .p program.

The protected author implementation executes successfully in MATLAB R2024a but differs numerically from the equation implementation. See probe_comparison.json and real_probe_comparison.json. The protected functions do not expose source code, and the cause of the numeric discrepancy remains unresolved. The output should be described as a paper-equation implementation, not a reproduced author-program value.
